# 🌍 AgriBridge AI

> **Africa's AI Farm Manager powered by Gemini**

AgriBridge AI is an AI-powered digital farm manager designed to help African farmers improve productivity, profitability, and sustainability through intelligent decision support.

---

## 🎯 Vision

To become Africa's leading AI Operating System for Agriculture, empowering millions of farmers with intelligent technology that improves productivity, profitability, and food security.

---

## 🚜 Key Features

- 🤖 AI Farm Manager
- 🌱 Crop Disease Detection
- 🌦️ Weather Intelligence
- 📈 Yield Prediction
- 🛒 Smart Marketplace
- 📒 Farm Record Management
- 💰 Financial Recommendations
- 📊 AI Business Insights
