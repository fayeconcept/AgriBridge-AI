# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

# AgriBridge AI

Version: 1.0

Author: Stephen Kuete

Status: Planning

---

# 1. Product Overview

AgriBridge AI is an AI-powered digital farm manager that helps African farmers make smarter farming decisions using artificial intelligence.

The platform combines AI, weather intelligence, crop disease detection, market access, financial insights, and farm management into one intelligent platform.

---

# 2. Vision

To become Africa's leading AI Operating System for Agriculture.

---

# 3. Mission

Empower every African farmer with an intelligent AI assistant that improves productivity, profitability, and sustainability.

---

# 4. Problem Statement

African farmers face many challenges:

- Poor access to agricultural experts
- Crop diseases
- Pest infestations
- Climate change
- Poor market access
- Low yields
- Limited financial inclusion

---

# 5. Target Users

Primary Users

- Smallholder farmers
- Commercial farmers
- Cooperatives

Secondary Users

- Agricultural Extension Officers
- Buyers
- Financial Institutions
- Government Agencies

---

# 6. Core Features

### AI Farm Manager

Provides intelligent farming recommendations.

### Disease Detection

Detect crop diseases from photos.

### Weather Intelligence

Provides weather forecasts and alerts.

### Smart Marketplace

Connects farmers directly with buyers.

### Farm Records

Stores farm activities digitally.

### Financial Insights

Tracks income and expenses.

### Dashboard

Displays farm analytics and reports.
